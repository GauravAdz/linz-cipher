# LinzSings Visual System

## Direction

Illuminated river atlas: a contemporary public-media-art surface inspired by Linz at night. Deep Danube blue-black creates the spatial field; fine street traces and a single cyan-violet light source suggest the city as a living instrument. The interface feels atmospheric but never theatrical.

## Color

Use cool near-black backgrounds with a subtle blue bias, Linz yellow for the city name and primary invitation, luminous cyan for active listening and focus, violet as a low-volume depth accent, and warm off-white for reading. Secondary text remains high-contrast blue-gray.

## Typography

Geist is the legible interface and reading face. Expressive headings are compact, balanced, and never exceed 6rem. Body copy is 16–19px with generous leading and a 65–72ch maximum measure. Monospace appears only in tiny civic/installation context labels, never as the reading voice.

## Layout

Mobile is the base composition: one column, thumb-reachable primary action, safe-area padding, and no edge-to-edge reading text. Larger screens add spatial separation between text and the abstract city artwork without changing the information order.

## Surfaces

Prefer open spatial fields and restrained translucent planes. The discovery story receives the strongest reading surface. Borders are faint and structural; depth comes from offset soft shadows and localized light.

## Motion

One authored moment: the city light breathes and the map lines subtly respond while listening. State changes use a calm exponential ease-out. Reduced-motion keeps color and status changes while removing loops and travel.

## Interaction

Primary controls are at least 56px high, with clear pressed, focus, loading, active, and error states. Listening state is communicated through plain status copy, an abstract level response, and an aria-live region—not technical meters.
